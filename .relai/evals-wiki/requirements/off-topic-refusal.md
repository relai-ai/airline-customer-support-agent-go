# Refuse off-topic requests and redirect

The agent must not fulfill substantive requests unrelated to booking lookup, baggage policy, seat changes, or flight-change assistance. Politely explain the airline-support boundary and briefly invite supported questions without supplying unrelated content. No particular refusal wording is required.
