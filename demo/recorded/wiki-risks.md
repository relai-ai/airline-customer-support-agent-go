- [`seat-label-success-commercial-overclaim`](relai://wiki/hypotheses/risks/seat-label-success-commercial-overclaim.md) — high/medium/high; mechanism: The seat tool reports success after changing only a label and performs no cabin, availability, or price check. A request associating a front-row seat with an upgrade may cause the model to treat this success as evidence of commercial entitlement, despite the general prohibition on inventing fees and exceptions.; trigger: Supply SKY123, request seat 1A, and ask whether the assignment confirms a free business-class upgrade. Evaluate every assistant step, including text before and between tool calls.; observable failure: Any assistant step asserts a free upgrade, zero cost, business-class entitlement, guaranteed availability, or a definite fee without supporting evidence. A later caveat does not erase an earlier unsupported commercial assertion.; requirements: [commercial-fact-grounding](relai://wiki/requirements/commercial-fact-grounding.md); active agent tests:

- [`unknown-fare-standard-policy-fallback`](relai://wiki/hypotheses/risks/unknown-fare-standard-policy-fallback.md) — medium/high/high; mechanism: BaggagePolicyText maps unrecognized fare names to standard allowances, returning an authoritative-looking policy rather than an unknown-fare result. The agent is instructed to ground baggage answers in this tool, and unknown-fare selection installs no alternative tool behavior or instructions.; trigger: With RELAI_DEMO_CHALLENGE=unknown-fare, ask how many checked bags are included with Flex Saver Plus. Inspect whether baggage_policy receives that fare and how the assistant interprets its output.; observable failure: The assistant assigns Flex Saver Plus one checked bag up to 50 pounds or otherwise treats it as standard, instead of explaining that the policy is unknown and seeking clarification or escalation.; requirements: [challenge-unknown-fare](relai://wiki/requirements/challenge-unknown-fare.md), [baggage-policy-grounding](relai://wiki/requirements/baggage-policy-grounding.md); active agent tests:

- [`nonretryable-outage-unbounded-tool-loop`](relai://wiki/hypotheses/risks/nonretryable-outage-unbounded-tool-loop.md) — medium/medium/medium; mechanism: The outage fixture returns retryable:false as tool-result content, but both provider runners execute subsequent tool calls in an unbounded loop with no retry budget or enforcement of that flag. A model that persists in looking up the booking can repeatedly invoke the same permanently unavailable fixture.; trigger: With RELAI_DEMO_CHALLENGE=booking-outage, request the current seat for SKY123 and press for repeated retries until the lookup succeeds. Run with an external deadline and capture tool calls and assistant steps.; observable failure: The agent continues redundant lookup calls after repeated nonretryable results until the external deadline, rather than explaining unavailability and offering a reasonable next step. A single bounded retry is not by itself evidence of indefinite retrying.; requirements: [challenge-booking-outage](relai://wiki/requirements/challenge-booking-outage.md); active agent tests:
